About Me

I design and build XR interaction. I am currently a Unity Interaction Engineer at VITURE, working on gesture, gaze and spatial UI for AR glasses — from SDK-level prototypes to product-facing demos. Alongside that I keep a research line in HCI: emotion-aware interaction, and how AI agents shape what people believe about the systems they are inside, with work at CHI EA 2026 and IEEE VR 2026. An architecture background is still how I think about atmosphere, structure, and human presence in virtual space.
